### About PerturbSigDB   

**PerturbSigDB** is a manually curated perturbation signature knowledgebase that integrateslarge-scale genetic and chemical perturbation signatures across diverse biological contexts.

 By organizing harmonized metadata, full gene-level results and pathway-level evidence,PerturbSigDB supports gene-level, pathway-level and signature-level exploration of perturbation-induced transcriptional programs.

 Overall, PerturbSigDB provides a unified resource for functional genomics, perturbationbiology, upstream regulator discovery, chemical modulator identification and mechanism-oriented interpretation.
